MX17 run budget: what it takes to run again, at LNL

Every component of the n_TOF apparatus, and what a run away from n_TOF needs for it: borrow or buy, a priority, a budgetary price, and what is still to be found out. Pick an option per item, or a whole scenario above; the totals, the map and the charts follow. Prices are EUR, 2026, before VAT, no labour. Orange confidence and ? mean "check this".
