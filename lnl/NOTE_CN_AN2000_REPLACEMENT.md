# Note: LNL may replace AN2000 and CN with one machine (recorded 2026-11-09)

**What was said.** Minutes of the LNL User Community Meeting, 12–13 Feb 2026 (signed by the User Board, 30 Mar 2026), Welcome from the Director:

> He suggested collecting input from users of small machines and proposed the possibility of organizing a dedicated meeting to discuss the future of small machines, with the possibility of replacing the AN2000 and CN accelerators with one machine, if there is interest in using them.

A. Selva's AN/CN talk at the same meeting adds: "Possible future efforts have been identified, especially in connection to a possible new machine." It also lists the AN 30-degree beam line as "used for X17 boson search".

Source: <https://www.lnl.infn.it/wp-content/uploads/Minutes_UserBoardCommunityMeeting_2025.pdf>

**Status.** A possibility raised for discussion. The minutes give no decision, date or specification, and we have not looked for anything later or asked anyone at LNL.

**Why it matters to the ⁸Be plan.**
- Risk: the plan assumes AN2000 or CN at about 1–4 µA and 1 MeV protons, on a multi-year scale. A replacement could change the beam lines, the schedule, or the machines themselves.
- Opportunity (our reading): "if there is interest" suggests user input counts, and an X17 programme is a use case for a new machine's specification, especially its DC current near 1 MeV.

**To ask Pierfrancesco, when outreach resumes.**
1. Has the dedicated meeting on the small machines happened or been scheduled?
2. Is a single replacement machine still under discussion, and on what timescale?
3. Should we submit the X17 requirements (energy range, DC current, target-station space for the arms) as user input?
