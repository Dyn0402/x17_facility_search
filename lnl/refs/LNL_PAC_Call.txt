Dear Colleagues, 
we inform you that the Program Advisory Committee (PAC) of the Laboratori Nazionali di Legnaro (LNL) of 
INFN will meet on October 8 – 10, 2025. 
The PAC will select experiments to be performed with the Tandem-ALPI-PIAVE accelerator complex and with 
the CN and AN2000 accelerators during the period October 2025 – July 2026.   
The deadline for the submission of proposals is September 3rd, 2025. 
Status of the Accelerators 
• The Tandem accelerator can be operated at terminal voltages up to 14 MV 
• PIAVE and ALPI will start operations from April 2026, with newly available 238U beams 
• The refurbished CN accelerator can be operated at terminal voltages up to 5.5 MV. Terminal voltages 
up to 6 MV  are possible but must be agreed with the Accelerator Division before proposal 
submission.  
• The AN2000 accelerator will be available in self -service mode: one energy change per day will be 
provided by the operating staff. 
An updated list of available beams and energies is accessible by following the links: 
https://www.lnl.infn.it/wp-content/uploads/Fasci_TAP.pdf 
We remind that the energies and intensities reported in the table are indicative ones: in selected cases higher 
energies may be achieved at the expense of beam intensities. The proponents are encouraged to contact 
pacbeams@lnl.infn.it for further details. 
Useful information about the CN and AN2000 accelerator may be found in: 
https://www.lnl.infn.it/wp-content/uploads/Beams_AN2000_CN.pdf 
A limited number of pulsed-beam days will be available in selected time periods at the CN accelerator. Users 
with approved pulsed beam shifts are advised to contact the Accelerator Division for details on machine 
performance before planning their shift in detail. 
The submission of proposals may be done here: https://www.lnl.infn.it/en/call-for-pac-proposals/  
You are encouraged to use the LaTeX or Word template files for the abstract and body of the proposal, 
available here: https://www.lnl.infn.it/en/call-for-pac-proposals/  
Please read carefully the details and instructions about the submission and evaluation procedures: 
https://www.lnl.infn.it/en/details-for-submissions/
