Funding the X17 search

Pierfrancesco suggested taking the feasibility studies to external funders. This page lists the EU, Italian and US schemes, says who in the group can apply for each and when, what each pays and asks for, and the next step. Rules and dates come from the call texts where they are published: check means a rule or date is not confirmed, ask means we lack a fact about a person. Updated .

Act this fortnight: the INFN fellowship for non-Italians closes on 12 Nov 2026. Dylan qualifies today and not after he signs the incarico. It pays €36 k gross a year for two years, against €28.8 k for one year, and LNL is an eligible site.

Dylan's best grant is STARS@UNIPD, then the ERC Starting Grant. STARS pays a PI salary and €175 k of research money for 30 months, is open to external PIs and is built as a rehearsal for the ERC. The 2027 edition is not announced yet (the last call opened in December). The ERC StG 2028 (~Oct 2027) gives €1.5 M + €1 M; 8.8 % of 2026 applicants won.

Money for the apparatus most likely comes from Italy: INFN CSN3 and PRIN, with Pierfrancesco as PI, plus Fondazione Cariparo's local excellence call. MSCA Staff Exchanges (15 Apr 2027) pays the travel between LNL, ATOMKI and a US group.

US federal grants need a US employer. The US routes are a US university PI (DOE and NSF both take proposals at any time now) and the Templeton Foundation (2027 cycle announced early 2027).
