Funding the X17 search

Pierfrancesco suggested taking the feasibility studies to external funders. This page lists the EU, Italian and US schemes, says who in the group can apply for each and when, and keeps the open questions. It is a first survey from public pages: check means a rule or date is not confirmed, ask means we lack a fact about a person. Updated .

Dylan is eligible for the main EU schemes. ERC and MSCA European Fellowships are open to any nationality: what counts is an EU host (INFN-LNL) and, for MSCA, where he lived before. US citizenship is no barrier.

Money for the apparatus most likely comes from Italy: INFN CSN3 and PRIN, with Pierfrancesco as PI. The only EU grant sized for an experiment is the ERC Starting Grant, Dylan's to write (2028 or 2029 call).

US federal grants need a US employer. The US routes are the Templeton Foundation, a US university PI, and Fulbright.

Moves the MSCA mobility count and the timeline. The bando says 12 months, renewable.
