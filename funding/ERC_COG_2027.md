## 1. Answer

**No. Dylan cannot apply to CoG 2027, and he cannot apply to the next CoG call (2028) either.**

- The CoG 2027 window is a PhD defence date **between 1 Jan 2012 and 31 Dec 2021** (more than 5 and at most 15 years
  before the cut-off date of 1 Jan 2027). WP 2027 §1.4.2.2, Table 4.
- Dylan's PhD (UCLA) is dated **10 Nov 2023**: 3 years 1 month before the cut-off. He would have needed to defend 1 year
  and 10 months earlier.
- The listed extensions (maternity, paternity and parental leave, long illness, national service, disability, clinical
  training, natural disasters, asylum, violence) **lengthen** the window for people past its far end. Nothing in the WP
  shortens the 5-year minimum.
- The next CoG he could enter is the **2029 call** (cut-off 1 Jan 2029: 5.1 years), but only if FP10 keeps the WP 2027
  windows. Under the old 7–12 year window it would be the 2031 call. The WP 2027 is the last of Horizon Europe, and the
  ERC "reserves the right to modify the restrictions … in its subsequent Work Programmes" **(check when the FP10 ERC
  work programme appears, ~spring 2027)**.
- **Dylan's ERC scheme is the Starting Grant.** The 2027 window is "> 0 and ≤ 10 years" since the defence, so he is
  eligible for every StG call up to the 2033 call (9.1 years at its cut-off). StG 2027 closed on 14 Oct 2026. The next is
  **StG 2028, expected ~mid Oct 2027** (first call under FP10; date and rules **(check)**). That is already the plan in
  `OPTIONS.md` §1.3.

So Pierfrancesco's "otherwise we have to wait for the next call" is right, but the next call to aim for is a **Starting**
Grant (Oct 2027), not a Consolidator.

## 3. CoG 2027 at a glance (WP 2027, Tables 1–3, 5)

| | |
|---|---|
| Call | ERC-2027-CoG, opened 24 Sep 2026, **deadline 12 Jan 2027** |
| Budget | €618.5 M, ~296 grants |
| Grant | up to €2 M for 60 months (pro rata if shorter) + up to €1 M additional funding (equipment, access to facilities, …); up to €2 M additional if the PI moves to the EU/AC from outside to take up the grant |
| Funding model | actual costs, 100 % of direct costs + 25 % flat indirect (subcontracts excluded) |
| PI time | ≥ 40 % of working time on the project, ≥ 50 % in the EU or an associated country |
| Host | one legal entity in the EU/AC (INFN-LNL would qualify); team members may come from anywhere |
| Proposal | B1 (Part I synopsis, CV and track record) and B2 (Part II, resources and time commitment) |
| Evaluation | Step 1 on B1, results by 16 Jul 2027; Step 2 on B2 + interview, results by 17 Dec 2027; grant signature ~8 Apr 2028 |
| Independence | "A Consolidator Grant PI should have already shown evidence of research independence" (§1.1.2) |
| Once per career | "A researcher may be the PI of only one ERC Consolidator Grant in their career" (being awarded one, not applying once) |

## 4. New WP 2027 restrictions that matter to the group

- **One eligible proposal per Work Programme** for each PI across all main-grant calls (StG, CoG, AdG, SyG, ERC Plus),
  and only one proposal under evaluation at a time. Anyone in our group who submits CoG 2027 cannot also submit SyG 2027
  (11 May 2027) or AdG 2027 (26 Aug 2027).
- **Resubmission blocks** (Table 6): a B or C at Step 1 in a 2026 StG/CoG call, or a C at Step 1 in a 2025
  StG/CoG/AdG call, bars all 2027 main calls. None of us has applied, so none applies.
- A PI who has **held** a StG (or CoG) cannot apply to the same scheme again.
- The StG 2027 call drew **7,710 applications** (+60 % on 2026) for ~373 grants, ~4.8 % if the number holds
  ([ERC, 23 Oct 2026](https://erc.europa.eu/news-events/news/starting-grants-2027-applications-facts-and-figures)).
  The wider windows mean much more competition. Expect StG 2028 to look like this, not like 2026's 8.8 %.

## 6. The INFN / LNL process (from the forwarded mails)

- LNL Servizio Fondi Esterni (Silvia Zanella, `fondiesterni.lnl@lnl.infn.it`) circulated the call (24 Sep 2026) with the
  WP 2027, the ERC *Information for Applicants* and APRE's *Preparing an ERC project*.
- Anyone applying with INFN as host must first tell the LNL Director (through the Servizio Fondi Esterni), get the
  approval of the Director and of the President of the relevant CSN (here CSN3), and then fill the INFN Servizio Progetti
  **manifestazione di interesse** form by **4 Dec 2026**. That is the internal gate for CoG 2027; no one in our group
  needs it.
- The material of INFN's national training course "Bandi ERC StG e CoG 2027" (July 2026) is on the INFN Progetti
  SharePoint (link in the mail). It covers the StG too and is worth reading in spring 2027 for the StG 2028.
- Expect the same internal gate (Director + CSN3 approval, an INFN interest form ~6 weeks before the deadline) for StG
  2028, around Aug–Sep 2027 **(check)**.
