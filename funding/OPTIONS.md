## 1. Short answer (updated 2026-10-27)

1. **Act now: the INFN fellowship for non-Italian citizens (bando 29217) closes on 12 Nov 2026**, with three
   reference letters due by 15 Nov. Dylan qualifies today (foreign PhD after 12 Nov 2018, not yet an INFN
   post-doc or fixed-term employee). LNL is one of the eligible sites. It pays **€36,000 gross a year for two
   years**, against the incarico's €28,762 for one year. Once he signs the incarico he cannot apply in later
   years. Open question: whether holding the incarico between an award (~spring 2027) and the fellowship start
   (by 1 Nov 2027) voids the fellowship. Ask INFN (`ac.dru.assegni.borse@lists.infn.it`) before accepting either.
2. **STARS@UNIPD Starting Grant is the best-fitting grant for Dylan before the ERC.** University of Padua,
   co-funded by Fondazione Cariparo: €175 k research money + a PI contract up to €42 k a year + up to €15 k
   mobility, 30 months, any nationality, external PIs welcome, PhD ≤ 5 y before 1 Jan of the call year. It is
   built as a rehearsal for the ERC (same B1/B2/interview format; the PI then must apply to the ERC). The host
   is a UNIPD department, not INFN. Editions in 2023 and 2025 opened in December; a 2027 edition is likely but
   not announced **(check)**.
3. **ERC Starting Grant 2028** (~mid Oct 2027, first call under FP10 **(check)**): €1.5 M + up to €1 M for
   equipment, 5 years. 8.8 % of 2026 applicants won (421 of 4,807). Host choice matters: UNIPD offers coaching
   (Talent@Unipd) and a possible permanent post, and Italian universities can hire ERC winners directly.
4. **MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowship 2027** (8 Sep 2027): about twice the incarico (employer cost ≈ €81 k a year).
   2027 is Dylan's only MSCA call for an Italian host. 9.6 % success in 2025.
5. **Apparatus money comes from Italy**: INFN CSN3 (the normal route), PRIN 2027 (~Apr–Jun 2027) and Fondazione
   Cariparo's excellence call (Pierfrancesco is the profile it asks for), all with Pierfrancesco as PI.
6. **Travel and network money**: MSCA Staff Exchanges (2027 call opens 15 Dec 2026, closes 15 Apr 2027) pays
   LNL ↔ ATOMKI ↔ a US group. ERC Synergy (11 May 2027) only if a multi-group X17 programme exists.
7. **US**: federal money still needs a US PI. DOE (open call to 30 Sep 2027) and NSF (26-523, any time) both take
   proposals at any time now. Templeton (≤ $2.5 M, BSM tabletop experiments) announces its 2027 cycle early 2027.
   Fulbright is closed to Dylan once he lives in Italy.

## 6. Deep dive: the schemes that matter most (2026-10-27)

### 6.1 INFN fellowship for non-Italian citizens (bando 29217)

- 20 fellowships in experimental physics for 2027/28 (resolution 17939 of 30 Sep 2026; bando dated 13 Oct 2026).
- **Eligible**: non-Italian citizens with a PhD from a foreign institution, awarded on or after 12 Nov 2018
  (extensions for parental leave, national service, illness; cap 12.5 y).
- **Excluded**: anyone who has held INFN fellowships for 24 months, and anyone who "already received a
  Post-Doctoral Fellowship or a fixed-term contract" with INFN. An incarico post-doc is a fixed-term employment
  contract, so Dylan is excluded after he signs it. On 12 Nov 2026 he has not.
- **Pay**: €36,000 gross a year for two years, travel to and from INFN reimbursed at the start and end, meal tickets.
- **Application** at reclutamento.dsi.infn.it: topic, up to two sites from Annex 1 (LNL and INFN Padova are
  listed), the foreign institution, a statement of research interests, CV, publication list, three referees
  (letters by 15 Nov 2026, 23:59 CET).
- **Selection**: track record, publications for the career stage, letters, fit to the topic.
- **Start**: by 1 Nov 2027; deferral requests possible.
- **What to decide**: if both are won, the fellowship (2 years, more pay) is the better contract. Whether INFN
  lets him hold the incarico until the fellowship starts is the one question to ask before signing anything
  **(check)**. The fellowship's tax treatment may change the net comparison **(check)**.

### 6.2 STARS@UNIPD (University of Padua)

- Fifth edition in 2025 (2023 and 2025 were held; both opened in December). Three lines: Starting (PhD ≤ 5 y
  before 1 Jan 2025, external PIs allowed), Consolidator (PhD 5–10 y, already at UNIPD), Wild Card (ERC "A" not
  funded with UNIPD as host).
- **Money**: research costs ≤ €175 k (PE) for 30 months; for a PI without a permanent UNIPD post, a contract up to
  €42 k gross a year (2025: an *assegno di ricerca* type B, which UNIPD may replace with an equivalent contract);
  a mobility allowance up to €15 k for PIs moving to Italy from abroad.
- **Obligation**: the PI submits an ERC proposal (or an equivalent scheme) afterwards with UNIPD as host.
- **Process (2025)**: deadline 21 Mar; eligibility check Apr; Step 1 on B1 (extended synopsis, CV, track record,
  10 pages) by end Jul; Step 2 on B2 (15 pages) by end Oct; 30-min interview Nov; results Dec; start 1 Feb 2026.
- **For Dylan**: a 2027 call (PhD after 1 Jan 2022) fits. He would hold a UNIPD contract in Physics and Astronomy
  and keep working at LNL through an INFN association **(check)**. Whether someone who moved to Italy in Dec 2026
  gets the mobility allowance **(check)**. Contact: talent.stars@unipd.it.

### 6.3 ERC (Work Programme 2027, the last of Horizon Europe)

| call | opens | deadline | budget | grants |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| StG 2027 | 22 Jul 2026 | 14 Oct 2026 (closed) | €594 M | ~373 |
| CoG 2027 | 24 Sep 2026 | **12 Jan 2027** | €618 M | ~296 |
| SyG 2027 | 17 Feb 2027 | **11 May 2027** | €500 M | ~48 |
| AdG 2027 | 27 May 2027 | **26 Aug 2027** | €798 M | ~311 |
| ERC Plus 2027 | 1 Jun 2027 | 1 Sep 2027 | €210 M | ~30 (≤ €10 M, 84 months) |

- New windows from 2027: StG = PhD in the 10 years before 1 Jan of the call year; CoG = 5–15 years.
- StG PIs must show independence (e.g. at least one important publication without the PhD supervisor).
- One ERC proposal per call; a B at step 1 can block the next call.
- Unfunded "A" proposals can be paid from EU funds a member state transfers; at UNIPD they enter the STARS Wild Card.
- **2028 under FP10**: the Commission proposed €175 bn for FP10 (2028–34), with the ERC kept; rules and dates for
  the 2028 StG come with FP10 and could slip, as the first Horizon Europe calls did in 2021 **(check)**. Expected
  StG 2028 deadline: mid Oct 2027 (Oxford, ANR).
- **Host**: INFN-LNL, or UNIPD with Talent@Unipd (internal selection and coaching; possible permanent post).
  Italian universities can hire ERC winners directly (L. 230/2005 art. 1 c. 9; e.g. Sapienza, Jan 2026).

### 6.4 MSCA (Work Programme 2026–27)

- PF unit costs (2026): living €6,350/month × country coefficient (Italy 95.3 % in the last table **(check)**),
  mobility €710 (+ family €660), research/training/networking €1,000, management €650. For Dylan that is about
  €81 k a year of employer cost against €40.7 k for the incarico.
- PF 2027: opens 7 Apr, closes 8 Sep 2027; the Commission added €70.75 M to the 2027 PF budget in Oct 2026.
  2025: 1,610 of 17,066 funded (9.6 %).
- Staff Exchanges 2027: opens 15 Dec 2026, closes 15 Apr 2027. 1–12 month secondments; unit ~€2,870 top-up +
  €1,300 research + €1,000 management per person-month (2026 guidance **(check)**). A same-sector (academic)
  consortium needs a partner in a non-associated third country: a US university fits.
- Choose Europe COFUND (second call, €51.25 M, 8 Dec 2026 – 6 Apr 2027) is for institutions' own postdoc
  programmes; INFN would have to apply.

### 6.5 Fondazione Cariparo, Ricerca Scientifica di Eccellenza

- €4 M per edition, three areas (natural sciences and engineering; life sciences; humanities and social sciences).
  The 2024 edition (9th) funded 14 of 229 proposals, 13 at UNIPD.
- PI: permanent or tenure-track (L. 79/2022) staff of research bodies in the provinces of Padova and Rovigo. LNL is
  in Legnaro (PD), so Pierfrancesco looks eligible; whether INFN counts as such a body **(check)** the regolamento.
- Two phases: executive summary + CV (2026: 22 Dec 2025 – 23 Feb 2026), full project (1 Jun – 7 Sep), results by
  December. Criteria include interdisciplinarity. Cariparo's 2026 plan (€65 M) confirmed the call; a 2027 edition
  **(check)**.

### 6.6 US federal routes, concretely

- DOE Office of Science: FY2027 continuation solicitation DE-FOA-0003665, posted 30 Sep 2026, applications any
  time to 30 Sep 2027 (some panels need a pre-application). A US university PI would request students, travel and
  hardware for the LNL search, with Dylan as a named foreign collaborator.
- NSF: NSF 26-523 (17 Aug 2026) replaced the PHY division solicitation; proposals any time, target dates on each
  program page (nuclear physics target date **(check)**).
