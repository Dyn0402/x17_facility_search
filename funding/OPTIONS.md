# Funding the X17 search: who can apply for what

Pierfrancesco suggested turning the feasibility studies into an application for external money,
EU or US. This file asks **who in the group is eligible for which scheme**, and when. It is a first
survey from public pages (2026-10-26). Rules change every call, so check each against the official
call text before planning around it. Facts I could not confirm are marked **(check)**; facts about
our own people I don't have are marked **(ask)**.

## 0. What we need to know about ourselves (ask)

Most of the answers below turn on four facts:

| fact | why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Dylan's PhD defence date** | ERC StG window (≤ 10 y), MSCA PF (≤ 8 y of experience), Montalcini (3–6 y), DOE Early Career (≤ 10 y) |
| **Where Dylan lived / worked in the last 36 months**, and the LNL start date | MSCA mobility rule (≤ 12 months in Italy in the 36 months before the deadline); Montalcini needs ≥ 3 y abroad *at application* |
| **Type of the LNL contract** (INFN *assegno di ricerca*, or fixed-term *ricercatore/tecnologo* (TD)) | PRIN PI needs a researcher/technologist post, not an assegno; assegni in Italy are capped at 6 years in total |
| **The Italian postdoc's PhD date and contract type, and Pierfrancesco's position** (INFN staff? university?) | Decides who can be PI on ERC CoG/AdG, PRIN, FIS |

## 1. Short answer (2026-10-26)

- **Yes, Dylan is eligible for the main EU schemes.** ERC and MSCA European Fellowships are open
  to any nationality. What counts is the host (in the EU or an associated country) and, for MSCA,
  where he lived before. US citizenship is not a barrier.
- **Best fit for Dylan: ERC Starting Grant** hosted by INFN-LNL. It is the only EU scheme sized for
  an experiment (€1.5 M + up to €1 M for equipment, 5 y). The 2027 call closed on 14 Oct 2026, so the
  next one is the 2028 call (FP10, deadline expected autumn 2027 **(check)**). The bar is very high.
  A year of preparation and a first result (the LNL ⁸Be run, or n_TOF 2026) would help a lot.
- **MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowship 2027** (deadline 8 Sep 2027, indicative): this pays Dylan's
  salary for 2 years at LNL. It is not experiment money (about €1 k/month for research costs). It is
  feasible **only if he has spent ≤ 12 months in Italy by 8 Sep 2027**, so a start at LNL after about
  Sep 2026 is fine. The Italian postdoc almost certainly fails the mobility rule for an Italian host.
- **The money for the apparatus will most likely come from Italy**: INFN CSN3 (the normal route for an
  LNL experiment, with Pierfrancesco or a staff member as *responsabile*), and PRIN or FIS with Pierfrancesco (or
  a TD researcher) as PI. Pierfrancesco is also the natural ERC Advanced/Consolidator PI if he wants one.
- **US federal money is closed to Dylan while he is employed in Italy.** DOE Early Career, NSF CAREER
  and NSF MPS-Ascend all need a US employer/host. The open US routes are **private foundations**
  (John Templeton Foundation: its 2026 physics call asked for exactly "new physics beyond the Standard
  Model" tabletop experiments, up to $2.5 M; next guidance in 2027), a **US university collaborator**
  who puts the X17 search into a DOE/NSF nuclear physics grant, and **Fulbright** (salary/travel, small).

## 2. EU schemes (Horizon Europe now, FP10 from 2028)

| scheme | who / host | size | eligibility that matters | next deadline | fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ERC Starting Grant** | single PI, host in EU/associated country (INFN-LNL qualifies) | up to €1.5 M over 5 y, + up to €1 M for major equipment or access to facilities (recent calls; FP10 sizes **(check)**) | any nationality, residence, employment; PhD ≤ 10 y before 1 Jan of the call year (2027 rule, new); PI spends ≥ 50 % of time in the EU/AC and ≥ 50 % on the project; one StG/CoG per career | 2027 call closed 14 Oct 2026; 2028 call under FP10, ~Oct 2027 **(check)** | **Best for Dylan** (or the Italian postdoc) |
| **ERC Consolidator** | as above | up to €2 M (+€1 M) | PhD 5–15 y before 1 Jan (2027 rule) | 2027 call ~Jan 2027 **(check)** | Italian postdoc or Pierfrancesco, if in window |
| **ERC Advanced** | senior PI | up to €2.5 M (+€1 M) | track record | ~Aug each year **(check)** | Pierfrancesco |
| **ERC Synergy** | 2–4 PIs | up to €10 M | — | ~Nov each year **(check)** | only with a wider X17 consortium (e.g. with ATOMKI, PADME or n_TOF people) |
| **MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowship, European** | Dylan + supervisor (Pierfrancesco) at LNL | 24 months of salary + ~€1 k/month research costs | any nationality; ≤ 8 y of research experience after the PhD; **mobility: ≤ 12 months resident / main activity in Italy in the 36 months before the deadline** | 2027: opens 7 Apr, closes **8 Sep 2027** (indicative) | Good for Dylan's salary; not hardware |
| **MSCA PF, Global** | outgoing to a non-associated country (e.g. US) + 12-month return | — | applicant must be an EU national or long-term resident (5 y) | — | Dylan not eligible |
| **MUR "Young Researchers / Seal of Excellence"** | MSCA or ERC proposals that scored above threshold but were not funded | national money at an Italian host | needs a Seal or an ERC B score first | after each call **(check)** | a fallback for the two above |
| **COST Action** | network of ≥ 7 countries | meetings and short visits only, ~€0.1–0.2 M/y | anyone | Oct each year **(check)** | a European X17 network; no hardware |
| **Transnational access** (EURO-LABS, ended Aug 2026) | beam time + travel at partner labs (LNL, n_TOF, …) | travel and subsistence | — | successor programme **(check)** | beam time at other facilities, not a grant |

**Dylan and the MSCA mobility rule.** The rule counts residence *or main activity* in Italy over
8 Sep 2024 – 8 Sep 2027. Work at CERN (France/Switzerland) and short beam-time trips do not count
for Italy. If Dylan starts at LNL in, say, Dec 2026, he has ~9 months by the deadline and is
eligible. A start before ~Sep 2026 makes him ineligible for an Italian host in 2027. An MSCA held
while already employed at LNL simply replaces the LNL salary for its two years.

## 5. Who should apply for what (proposal)

1. **Pierfrancesco**: INFN CSN3 funding for the LNL ⁸Be run (the hardware, beam time, target). PRIN 2027
   as PI, with Dylan and the Italian postdoc as members. ERC Advanced if he wants to lead a large
   programme.
2. **Dylan**: ERC Starting Grant 2028 at INFN-LNL (start writing spring 2027, for the ~Oct 2027 deadline), and an
   MSCA PF 2027 at LNL as a salary backstop (if the mobility rule holds). Templeton OFI 2027 with
   LNL/INFN as grantee. Montalcini only if a call opens before the move.
3. **The Italian postdoc**: ERC Starting/Consolidator (depending on the PhD date) and an INFN young-researcher
   grant. MSCA only with a host outside Italy.
4. **US**: find one US university PI to carry an X17 line in a DOE NP / NSF grant (students, travel).

The feasibility material that would go into any of these: the facility comparison (`README.md`), the
LNL study (`lnl/`, reach in ~2 weeks of beam at 1 µA), and the world surveys (`survey/`). The ERC
panel would be PE2 (fundamental constituents of matter).
